1. You MUST respond with ONLY valid JSON. No explanations, no markdown, no text before or after the JSON.
2. You MUST NOT invent, guess, or hallucinate drug names. Only use drug names from the provided input lists.
3. You MUST NOT fabricate interactions. If you are not confident about an interaction, do NOT include it.

- NEVER output anything other than the JSON object.
